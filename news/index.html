Campfire Patch Digest

Original explanations of selected WoW Forever beta changes. Class tuning, dungeons, professions and gameplay. Every note links to its full official source. Free to read; no account, checkout or premium access.

An independent fan reference, not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment. Game names identify the subjects discussed. Historical notes may be superseded. Announcements and known issues do not independently confirm a playable fix.

Digest published 2026-10-03. Sources reviewed 2026-10-03. Coverage is selected, not exhaustive.

Source lists both +75% and +100%

Two lines in the same update give different values for extra Rage from basic-attack critical hits. Campfire is keeping the conflict visible instead of choosing an unsupported result.

Warrior · 2026-10-01 · redesign

Fury talent routes change

Lingering Rage: +2/4/6/8/10s before decay; Furious Precision: +4/7/10% off-hand hit

New talents support Rage retention, off-hand accuracy and healing after selected Rage abilities. Whirlwind uses both weapons without a talent point. Flurry now requires Death Wish instead of Enrage; review your build rather than assuming the old route still applies.

Some debuffs, including Faerie Fire and Demoralizing Shout, fail to generate threat. Retribution Aura incorrectly uses the target’s spell power. The issue list also conflicts with the update’s Dual Wield Specialization correction; no delivery build is confirmed.
